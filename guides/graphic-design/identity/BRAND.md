# BRAND — TA Brand Foundation

**Status:** v0.3 · 12 October 2026 — personal umbrella/site architecture restored from 27 September conversation; Portfolio and Studio at parity  
**Purpose:** The short strategic brief behind the TA visual identity. This is **not** a claim that every proposed positioning or line of copy has already been approved.

> **Central proposition (PROPOSED):** TA brings architectural design and computational thinking together to make **distinctive, intelligible and buildable work**—and to develop reusable methods, tools and knowledge from that work.

## 1. What TA is

**ESTABLISHED**

**TA / Teodor Andonov** is the personal umbrella identity of an architect, computational designer and researcher. It gathers authored architecture, professional experience, independent projects, computational tools, research, experiments, writing and future ventures.

**APPROVED / historically confirmed:** TA / teodorandonov.com is the *personal umbrella and website*, not one professional studio. Under it sit Portfolio, Studio/practice, Notes, Apps/products and other projects. Portfolio and Studio have equal prominence, but are not the only top-level destinations. This was articulated on 27 September and reaffirmed 12 October 2026. It is **one personal umbrella with several expressions**, not necessarily one company, one agency, or one narrow service:

- **Portfolio** — selected personal work and credited professional experience, with roles, employers and authorship made explicit.
- **Studio / independent practice** — specialist services, collaborations and commissioned work. **Method / Studio Method** is now the owner's leading *provisional* studio name; earlier shortlist favoured METOD, not a finalized trademark.
- **Notes** — writing, research, short notes, ideas and longer articles.
- **Apps / projects** — tools, configurators, experiments and projects such as Balkan House; each may acquire its own subsite, product identity and later independent domain.
- **Future initiatives** — can join this umbrella without forcing everything into Studio or Portfolio.

The identity must work for an **individual author today** and an expandable practice tomorrow. That scalability must not erase the two equally prominent public routes:

1. **Explore work** — authored projects, archive, research, notes, tools, transparent project credits and ongoing investigations.
2. **Work together** — an intelligible practice offer, capabilities, engagement models, relevant evidence and a way to discuss commissioned work.

**Equal prominence applies to Portfolio and Studio as first-class destinations**, without erasing Notes, Apps and Projects. It means equal access and credibility—not equal content volume, identical commercial status or invented client commissions. A separate studio or product may ultimately establish its own domain and brand while TA remains the personal parent/link source.

**Important distinction:** TA is the umbrella. A separately named independent practice may eventually sit beneath it; a future practice name has **not** been decided. *Andonov Atelier* currently refers to a particular personal architectural project, not the settled umbrella or company brand.

## 2. Positioning

**PROPOSED core positioning**

> TA is Teodor Andonov's personal home for architectural work, an emerging independent studio, research, notes and digital tools. These related activities connect design authorship with computational methods—from exploratory ideas to systems that can be tested, developed and, where relevant, built.

**APPROVED positioning structure:** TA is the umbrella; **Portfolio** and **Studio** are comparably prominent first-class sections alongside **Notes** and **Apps / Projects**. The proposed sentence above is descriptive working copy, not an approved tagline. The studio's work/offer must be actionable but does not own the personal portfolio. Notes/apps have their own homes. No personal experiment is described as a fee-paying studio commission.

**Short public introduction (PROPOSED):**

> TA is the work of Teodor Andonov—an architect, computational designer and researcher working across architecture, digital methods and design systems.

**Established descriptive lockup:**  
**TA — Teodor Andonov**  
**Design, Architecture, Computation**

Use the above descriptor as the accepted historical identity wording. “Architecture / Computation / Research” is a useful **contextual description**, but it has not formally replaced the lockup.

### The promise

**PROPOSED:** *Ideas should not stop at compelling form. The design logic should be clear enough to understand, test, develop, share and, where relevant, build.*

This is a **standard of work**, not an assertion that every published concept has already been built or verified.

### Point of difference

The intersection is the differentiator, not any one buzzword:

1. **Design authorship:** spatial and visual judgment; a recognisable design voice, not only automated output.
2. **Computational fluency:** parametric modelling, code, systems thinking and reproducible workflows used to explore and resolve problems.
3. **Technical reality:** façades, geometry, materials, fabrication and performance considered as part of design, not as a late-stage correction.
4. **Transferable knowledge:** useful tools, methods, documentation and repeatable systems remain after each project.

**Avoid overclaiming:** this is the intended way of working, supported by prior experience and research. It is not a guarantee of a particular deliverable, certification, construction outcome or commercial track record.

These are **subject areas**, not four identical product categories and not a final priced service menu.

- Clearly scoped/fixed-output packages.
- A subscription arrangement.
- Traditional/custom contracts.

These are offering experiments, **not** assertions that each mode is already launched. The proposed first contact action is a **15-minute introductory call**.

**Strategic filter (PROPOSED):** prioritize work where **design quality and computational/technical leverage reinforce one another**. Avoid marketing TA as generic graphic design services, generic AI automation, or a full-service multidisciplinary firm without supporting delivery capacity.

## 5. Identity character

Six pairs define the personality and visual/verbal behavior:

| Trait | Express as | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Precise** | Clear geometry, consistent rules, specific language | Arbitrary styling or vague claims |
| **Inventive** | Unexpected arrangements, meaningful experiments | Novelty without purpose |
| **Quietly confident** | Generous space, strong work, restrained claims | Corporate superlatives and boastful sales copy |
| **Systematic** | Relationships, evidence, process and repeatability | Cold bureaucracy or diagrams for their own sake |
| **Human** | Legible explanations, discernible authorship, warmth | Impersonal engineering-only identity |
| **Open** | Share research, methods and learning; credit collaborators | Artificial mystique or pretending to know everything |

**Positioning tension to preserve:** exacting **but** not sterile; creative **but** not arbitrary; technological **but** not gimmicky; professional **but** not anonymous.

## 6. Verbal identity

**PROPOSED voice:** calm, direct, thoughtful, specific and slightly curious. Write as a capable architect explaining work to another intelligent person.

- State what was done, why, with what constraints, and what changed.
- Prefer **verbs and evidence** to adjectives: design, test, compare, build, measure, prototype, document.
- Keep headlines concise; let diagrams, models and project artifacts provide proof.
- Be candid about **idea vs research vs prototype vs built work**.
- Use **“I”** where authorship is personal, **“we”** only when a genuine team/practice is involved. Do not invent a large studio.
- Clearly credit employers, teams, collaborators and project stages. Do not portray employer projects as independent TA commissions.
- Write for a technical but not exclusively technical audience; explain specialist terms when they materially affect understanding.
- Avoid “revolutionary”, “disruptive”, “AI-powered” as default identity claims, generic manifesto language and ornate architectural jargon.

### Working message hierarchy

1. **Who:** TA / Teodor Andonov — architect, computational designer, researcher.
2. **What:** architecture, computation and design systems.
3. **How:** design judgment + explicit methods + iterative tests + technical resolution.
4. **Proof:** selected projects, working prototypes, diagrams, research and transparent credits.
5. **Action:** give Portfolio **Explore work** and Studio **Work together** equal prominence, and retain clear independent routes to **Notes** and **Apps / Projects**.

## 7. Relationship to graphic identity

The graphic language should **embody** the positioning, not merely decorate it.

- **Authorship + systems:** tight underlying grid / shared-unit logic with room for expressive variation.
- **Precision + curiosity:** monochrome editorial typography paired with distinctive generative geometry.
- **Continuity:** one visual grammar shared across website, printed reports, diagrams and applications.
- **Credibility:** showcase truthful project images and annotated evidence, not only abstract “tech” textures.
- **Knowledge as output:** labels, captions, indexes, process diagrams and reusable graphic assets have legitimate roles.
- **Ownable symbol:** crisp, fused rounded-module glyphs (A) can form the distinctive signature; softer mineral fields (B) can establish atmosphere. The final logo remains open.

**Visual requirements already specified elsewhere:** Geist Mono preferred; Latin + Bulgarian Cyrillic; grayscale-first; recursive Card/Object grammar; shared unit; responsive grid; A/B imagery. See [DESIGN.md](../DESIGN.md) and [IMAGERY.md](../IMAGERY.md) for detailed constraints. Do not duplicate changing numeric tokens here.

  teodorandonov.com/studio/ → independent Method studio domain

These are **navigation concepts**, not locked slugs, domain registrations or product launches. See [SITE_ARCHITECTURE.md](SITE_ARCHITECTURE.md).

The umbrella site should allow editorial and professional content to coexist, but not misrepresent speculative projects, personal architecture work, employer work or future product ideas as completed independent client commissions.

AI may improve the **workflow and infrastructure** but should not be treated as the public defining promise. Architectural ideas and working systems are the result; the tools are means.

## 9. Success criteria for Stage 01

This brief is **ready to support Stage 02 visual-direction boards** when:

- The TA umbrella vs independent-practice distinction is respected.
- The agreed lockup/descriptor is preserved and any revised public wording is explicitly chosen.
- The audiences and four fields of work are intelligible.
- The distinguishing combination of **authorship + computation + technical reality + reusable knowledge** is legible.
- The voice is consistent and honest about project credits and status.
- The visual direction is tested against actual identity uses—not only a logo on white.

**Not required before Stage 02:** deciding a separate studio name, a final tagline, every offer/package price or the final logomark.
